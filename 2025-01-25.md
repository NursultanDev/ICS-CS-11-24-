# IoT
The Internet of Things (IoT) is a web of linked devices that gather, share, and handle data to boost automation, efficiency, and call-making. IoT uses sensors, communication tech, and cloud computing to enable smart apps in many fields, such as health, transport, smart homes, and factory control. By making real-time watch and control possible, IoT boosts output, cuts costs, and makes user experiences better. But, issues like security, scalability, and device compatibility are still big concerns. As IoT keeps growing, advances in AI, edge computing, and 5G are shaping its next uses.
