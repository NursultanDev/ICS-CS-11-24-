Artificial Intelligence (AI) is like giving computers a brain—one that can learn, adapt, and even make decisions. It’s behind the smart assistants that answer our questions, the filters that sort our emails, and the algorithms that recommend what to watch next. AI helps doctors detect diseases, businesses automate tasks, and even cars drive themselves. While it’s making life easier in many ways, it also raises big questions—how do we keep it fair, safe, and under control? AI isn’t just about robots and sci-fi; it’s already shaping our world, and its future depends on how we choose to use it.
