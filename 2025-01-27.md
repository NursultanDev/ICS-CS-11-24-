Cybersecurity is all about keeping our digital lives safe. From protecting personal data to stopping hackers from breaking into systems, it’s like the digital version of locking your doors at night. Every time we shop online, log into social media, or use a banking app, cybersecurity is working behind the scenes to keep our information secure. But as technology evolves, so do cyber threats—hackers get smarter, and attacks get more complex. That’s why cybersecurity is a never-ending game of cat and mouse, constantly adapting to stay one step ahead. Whether it's strong passwords, encryption, or AI-powered threat detection, cybersecurity helps us navigate the digital world safely.
